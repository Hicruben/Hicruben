# Hi, I'm Jerry Chen

I build practical, source-checked guides and calculators.

## Travel

- [Which Park Pass](https://whichparkpass.com): Compare US national park entry costs, including the $100 nonresident fee, for your parks, dates and group.
- [PowerBank Pass](https://powerbankpass.com): Check power bank capacity, count, use and storage rules for a flight, from official airline sources with dated checks.
- [China in Reach](https://chinainreach.com): Illustrated city and attraction guides, source-linked visitor information and an editable itinerary planner for independent China trips.

## Outdoors and home

- [Balcony Solar Atlas](https://balconysolaratlas.com): State-by-state plug-in solar rules, a production and payback calculator, and safety guides for US renters and homeowners.
- [COLD PSI](https://coldpsi.com): Guides, a calculator and a check sheet for cold tire pressure, TPMS warnings and each tire's placard value.
- [Lawn Seed Planner](https://lawnseedplanner.com): Read a seed label, calculate material and bags, and follow Minnesota-scoped timing, preparation and establishment guides.

## Hobbies

- [LoRa Mesh Atlas](https://lorameshatlas.com): Source-checked guides and a device database for Meshtastic and MeshCore LoRa mesh radios in the US.
- [HerpWise](https://theherpwise.com): Care guides for leopard geckos, bearded dragons and crested geckos, with original diagrams and every fact linked to its source.
- [Mahj Atlas](https://mahjatlas.com): A guide to American mahjong: compare tile sets, learn the rules and the Charleston, and find lessons and open play nearby.
- [Mat & Margin](https://matandmargin.com): Plan picture-mat openings, check signatures and compare board sizes, with illustrated guides and a printable measurement note.
- [Playtime Base](https://playtimebase.com): An unofficial Escape from Playtime fan site with guides, a crew card builder, a daily quiz and original fan games.
